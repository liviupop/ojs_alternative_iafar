## Abstract EN
The study approaches the mythical ethnogony and the historical ethnogenesis of the Romanians. Aer analysing the speci c terminology which includes concepts like: ethnogony, ethnogenesis, eponym heroes, ethnonyms, endonym and exonym, mythonym, we reveal the state of art in the eld of the researches dedicated to the topic of the foundations. We show the historical and mythical sources of the Romanian “dismounting” (descălecat) and the evolution of the scholars’ preoccupations dedicated to the genesis of the Romanians. In the end, we explain the mythical signi cance attributed to Romanian ethnogony, comprising the utilitarian function, the spiritual meaning, the moral responsibility and the transcendental sense connected with the foundation of the people, as well as the historical imaginary of the Romanian ethnogenesis, insisting on: the ontological value of the name by which people found their existence, the politicization of geography and the nationalization of geographical dierences, the teleological interpretation with regard to the Romanian ethnogenesis and the Vlach “colonization”.

## Keywords EN
ethnogonical foundation myths, historical ethnogenesis, Vlach “colonization”, signi cance of the Romanian mythical ethnogony, values linked to the Romanian ethnogenesis
