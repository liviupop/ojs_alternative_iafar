## Abstract EN
The following pages are a brief presentation of an interview with Virgiliu Florea (University Professor in Folklore Studies and scienti c researcher at the Cluj Folklore Archive). The questions addressed in the interview, as well as the responses, are summarized systematically. The aims of the interview have been to collect autobiographical data, focusing on mostly the researcher’s scienti c career. References to the institutional millieus in which Folklore Studies have been present, before and aer 1989, as well as an analysis on the place of Folklore studies among other socio-human sciences (in various decades of the above mentioned periods) are on the agenda.
